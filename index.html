About Me

Three crafts, one way of thinking

I'm a freelance creative and developer working across motion design, mobile apps, and — through my BS in Artificial Intelligence — agentic AI.

My work usually starts the same way regardless of the medium: understand what a story, a user, or a task actually needs, then build the thing that delivers it. Sometimes that's a short-form video edited to hold attention for fifteen seconds. Sometimes it's a Flutter app someone uses every day. Right now, I'm also deep in coursework and hands-on practice with agentic AI — building with CrewAI, LangChain, and the OpenAI SDK — because I want automation to be part of what I offer clients, not just a side interest.
